# EndureLuxe Brand and Product Authority

Status: governing V1 product boundary

## Identity

EndureLuxe is an independent consumer physical-resilience and consistency brand. It shares a founder with Abraham of London and Alomarada Ltd but is not an operating arm, subsidiary, product or methodology implementation of either estate.

## Retired claims

The following historical ideas are not authorised for the new site:

- “Luxury fitness equipment” as the organising proposition.
- “Two domains, one operating system.”
- EndureLuxe as Abraham of London doctrine applied to the body.
- Physical training adherence as evidence of institutional or professional competence.
- Alomarada and EndureLuxe as sister operating arms inside an AOL holding structure.

Historical copies may remain in archived evidence but must not return to public product architecture.

## Product

The first commercial product is **The 45**, a 45-day consistency practice. It sells structure and accountability around participant-selected appropriate activity; it does not sell diagnosis, clinical exercise prescription or guaranteed body transformation.

The 45 is also explicitly **not** an AI coach or a supplement programme. Those are excluded product categories for V1 and must not be introduced by implication, copy, feature design or implementation without a later governed product decision.

## Commercial sequence

Audience → The 45 → Endure Club → Endure Days → optional Field Kit products after demand.

Inventory-heavy retail is not the V1 model.

## Commercial and data-control implementation decisions

The following decisions were adopted in merged PR #1 and are part of the governing V1 product boundary rather than PR-history-only context:

- Registration and payment use explicit adapters. No fake checkout is permitted.
- Preview registration must not claim durable persistence when no registration endpoint is configured.
- Preview and non-canonical hosts must remain `noindex`; pre-launch static robots defaults fail closed until canonical launch is explicitly qualified.
- V1 founding registration intentionally avoids health/special-category data.
- Marketing consent is separate and optional.

Implementation may strengthen these controls, but may not silently weaken or bypass them.

## Data boundary

V1 founding registration intentionally avoids health/special-category data. Programme administration and optional marketing consent are separate purposes. EndureLuxe participant data must not flow automatically into Abraham of London research/publication or Alomarada commercial systems.

## Source and build authority

GitHub repository `AbrahamofLondon/endureluxe.com` is the durable source authority once V1 is merged to `main`. Lovable is the preferred build/edit/deployment plane and should be connected to this repository when workspace build credits are available.

Canonical domain target: `endureluxe.com`.
