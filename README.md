# EndureLuxe

**Stay capable.**

EndureLuxe is an independent consumer physical-resilience and consistency brand. Its first commercial product is **The 45**, a structured 45-day practice designed to help busy adults make movement, strength and recovery durable parts of normal life.

## Governing brand boundary

- **EndureLuxe Limited** — independent consumer brand focused on durable physical capability, consistency and community.
- **Abraham of London** — separate intellectual, research and publishing estate.
- **Alomarada Ltd** — separate institutional commercial firm.

The brands may share a founder and a standard of disciplined craft, but EndureLuxe is not an Abraham of London operating arm, an Alomarada product, or an application of a shared corporate methodology.

## Retired positioning

The historical description **“Luxury fitness equipment”** is obsolete. The legacy `alomarada.com/endureluxe` and former umbrella-site copy are source history only and must not define the new product architecture.

## Core proposition

> Fitness that survives real life.

The 45 is not a medical programme, transformation challenge, AI coach, supplement programme, or personalised clinical exercise prescription. It provides a structure for consistency: baseline, minimum viable commitments, movement, strength, recovery, daily check-ins, weekly reflection, a restart protocol, accountability and a final 45-day record.

## Commercial sequence

1. Audience and editorial trust
2. **The 45** founding cohort — £99 one-off introductory price
3. Endure Club recurring membership after demand is proved
4. Endure Days / in-person community events later
5. Carefully selected physical Field Kit products only after demand exists

No inventory-heavy retail launch is authorised by this repository.

## Build architecture

GitHub is intended to become the durable source authority. Lovable is the preferred build/edit/deployment plane once the workspace has sufficient build credits and is connected to this repository.

Long-term canonical domain: `endureluxe.com`.

A temporary Lovable-hosted production origin or an Abraham of London subdomain may be used during transition, but neither changes EndureLuxe's independent brand/data/commercial boundary.

## Product principle

**Move. Strengthen. Recover. Return. Continue.**

The programme succeeds when the practice can survive ordinary life.
